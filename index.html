How to use

Every section of the app has a help icon (?) that explains the inputs, what to do and what not to do. Read it before your first use.

Try to use a balanced army most of the time: no extra flying, melee, ranged or mounted bonuses, and preferably no difference between the health and strength of Guardsmen, Specialists, Monsters and Engineers (extra strength of Monsters is fine).

  1. Pick the captains and hero who will go on the attack and give them fighting equipment. Captains and hero who do not take part in the attack can't be fighting captains and get no fighting equipment.
  2. Click Attack on the enemy (epic or player) and choose your fighting squad. The game shows your Leadership, Authority and Dominance numbers for the calculator at the bottom of the screen. Type them into Army capacity.
  3. Go to the Barracks to find your bonuses. If your fighting squad changes (e.g. change of captain, level up, added talents) type bonuses again.
    1. Click Guardsmen, click on one and scroll down to the bonuses section. Type the chance to deal double damage, the chance to strike two squads and the strength of your entire army against epic monsters (or the health and strength against players, if you attack a player) into Army bonuses (%), Entire army section, once. Type the Health and Strength bonuses in Category bonuses (%), Guardsmen section. Now click Specialists, click on one, scroll to its bonuses and type the Health and Strength bonuses in Category bonuses (%), Specialists section. Repeat for Monsters and Engineers.
    2. Go to the Academy, section Monsters boost, and based on the research parts you have completed, click I · 19 %, II · 50 % or III · 100 % in Monster boost research bonuses (%). If a part is incomplete, you need to add up the health and strength numbers for each monster type, so it is better to complete the research.
    3. For Rare bonuses (%), click on the help first, as you probably do not need to fill anything here.
      • If you use a lot of mercenaries or scouts, set the Special troop type bonuses.
      • If you use captains with effects on only one part of the army, set the Combat type bonuses.
      • If you fight with standard or tournament equipment, set the Category bonuses too.
  4. Pick your tiers, then click the Tier check button and adjust the tiers if you like the suggestions.
  5. Scroll to Calculation, pick a view that suits you, go to Hitting and type your army numbers into the game.

If the attack in the game looks wrong, click the Battle report button in the Calculation section.

  1. Type your total food production into Food prod. and pick the Revive you want.
  2. Click Preparation in the Action section: it shows how many units to train per stack at once for the maximum hit per training.
  1. Click Compare in the Action section.
  2. Type the units of your own stacks, or upload screenshots of the in-game battle report showing all of them.
  3. See how your stacks differ from the Krappy ones.
